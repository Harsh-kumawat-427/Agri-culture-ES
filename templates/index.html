Four Pillars of Agricultural Precision

Combining soil chemistry, environmental science, supervised machine learning, and forward-chaining rule engines.

Pillar 1

Soil Chemistry & Macronutrient Dynamics

Soil cation exchange capacity and nutrient availability are strongly dictated by pH chemistry and textural classification (Loamy, Clayey, Sandy, Black Cotton, Alluvial, Red). The expert system evaluates Available Nitrogen (N), Available Phosphorus (P), and Available Potassium (K) against crop-specific physiological thresholds.

Nitrogen (N): 0 – 140 kg/ha range evaluated
Phosphorus (P): 5 – 145 kg/ha evaluated
Potassium (K): 5 – 205 kg/ha evaluated
Reaction (pH): 3.5 – 9.5 acidity/alkalinity bounds

Transparent & Explainable AI (XAI)

Why combining Machine Learning with Rule-Based reasoning outperforms black-box models for agriculture.

Explainable Reasoning vs Traditional Guesswork
  • No Black-Box Guesswork: Every fertilizer recommendation is accompanied by the exact IF-THEN rule ID (e.g. R01, R15) that triggered it.
  • Dual Metric Dosages: Calculations are rendered simultaneously in kilograms per hectare (kg/ha) and kilograms per acre (kg/acre).
  • Soil pH Reaction Correction: Automatically detects acid/alkaline conditions and prescribes corrective agricultural limestone or gypsum.
  • Official Printable Records: Generates tamper-evident consultation records saved in the SQLite audit log with instant print/PDF formatting.
