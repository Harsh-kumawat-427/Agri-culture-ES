Dosage Calculation Rule Formulation

The prescribed fertilizer dosage is computed by comparing the crop's ideal requirement against the current soil nutrient level to derive the nutrient deficit gap:

Dosage (kg/hectare) = Nutrient Gap (kg/ha) ÷ [ Active Nutrient Percentage ÷ 100 ]
Example 1 (Urea - 46% N): If Rice ideal Nitrogen is 80 kg/ha and current soil is 60 kg/ha, Gap = 20 kg/ha. Dose = 20 ÷ 0.46 = 43.5 kg/ha.
Example 2 (DAP - 46% P): If Chickpea ideal Phosphorus is 68 kg/ha and soil is 20 kg/ha, Gap = 48 kg/ha. Dose = 48 ÷ 0.46 = 104.3 kg/ha.
Example 3 (MOP - 60% K): If Grapes ideal Potassium is 200 kg/ha and soil is 150 kg/ha, Gap = 50 kg/ha. Dose = 50 ÷ 0.60 = 83.3 kg/ha.
