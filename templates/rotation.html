Principles of Temporal Crop Recommendation & Rotation

How our expert system synchronizes soil fertility with seasonal thermal units and harvest intervals.

1. Harvest Gap Window
Fallow Period Match

When farmers rotate between major staples (e.g., Rice-Wheat), short fallow gaps (60-85 days) open. Recommending short-cycle legumes prevents land idling and maximizes annual farm revenue.

2. Thermal Units (GDD)
Growing Degree Days

Every crop demands a physiological base temperature. The system guarantees that your seasonal ambient temperature provides the necessary degree days for vegetative and flowering stages.

3. Soil Condition Match
Texture & pH Dynamics

Soil texture (sandy, loamy, clayey, black) determines water holding capacity and root aeration, matching moisture-loving crops to heavy soils and drought-tolerant pulses to sandy soils.

4. Rotation Synergy
Nitrogen Regeneration

Alternating deep-rooted legumes with fibrous-rooted cereals naturally fixes 30-50 kg/ha atmospheric Nitrogen, disrupts insect pest cycles, and prevents soil exhaustion.
